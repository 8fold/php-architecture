## Peace, not perfection.

Practical software for the ordinary matters of life. Built with neurodivergent
adults in mind, Mastering the Mundane offers a place to notice what is happening
without turning daily life into a performance.

<article>

### Time Journal

A clearer picture of time. Not another measure of worth.

Record time spent on work, rest, meals, conversations, and the things in
between. Start and stop an activity as it happens, log an entry afterward,
or adjust times when needed.

Use broad activity names or add nested activities when more detail is helpful.
The record is information, not a grade; it does not have to account for every
minute to be useful.

*Time Journal records what is entered; it does not automatically track activity
away from the app.*
